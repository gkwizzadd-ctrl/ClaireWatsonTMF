How It Works
Everything on this site, at a glance

This site is how Claire helps telecom and satellite operators navigate TM Forum's Open Digital Architecture (ODA) — here's what each part does.

What each feature does
  1. Expertise, About, Testimonials — jump straight to a section via the nav to see Claire's ODA specialisms, her background, and what other members say about working with her.
  2. Contact — a short form to get in touch directly. Submitting it can also unlock a personalised, downloadable Engagement Plan questionnaire tailored to your organisation.
  3. Ask Claire's Team (chat bubble, bottom corner) — a live chat that answers ODA questions and can hand you straight to the contact form when you're ready to talk.
  4. 💡 Thought of the Month (bulb button) — a short video with Claire's current perspective on ODA transformation.
  5. 📊 Case Studies — real TM Forum member training journeys, starting with Tata Communications Transformation Services (TCTS), including a self-assessment you can take against your own organisation.
  6. 📄 Documents — the Benefits and Roadmap briefs, TM Forum's own member Case Studies, and an interactive ODA Case Study of our own: select a component from the real ODA canvas and watch it untangle a typical operator's legacy BSS/OSS stack.
  7. 🎮 Games & Quiz — ODA Chess and the TMF AI Quiz, two lighter ways to learn ODA concepts.

Everything here is free to explore.

About Claire

Engagement Manager.
ODA advocate.
Industry connector.

As Engagement Manager at TM Forum, Claire Watson sits at the intersection of telecoms standards, digital transformation, and industry collaboration. She works with communications service providers around the world to help them understand, adopt, and derive real business value from the Open Digital Architecture.

With deep expertise across the ODA framework — from eTOM process design and SID information modelling to Open API implementation and AI-native canvas deployment — Claire translates the complexity of industry standards into practical, achievable transformation programmes.
